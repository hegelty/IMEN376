2) Production strategy, push-pull boundary, and forecasting
Production strategy. The Cass family is best modeled as MTS because brewing lead time is long, demand is repetitive, and retail service requires immediate availability.
Push-pull boundary. The push portion includes brewing and tank scheduling up to matured beer; the pull portion begins when downstream channels draw from finished inventory. Because packaging is excluded, the practical boundary is just after maturation (and after de-alcoholization for Cass 0.0).
Forecasting view. Forecast error is likely smallest for flagship Cass, moderate for Cass Light, and largest for Cass 0.0 because non-alcoholic demand is more promotion-sensitive and less stable.
